# Challenge – Aggregate functions with employees

## Goal
The goal of this exercise was to practice using aggregate functions to summarize data in a table.

## Reasoning

1. To find the employee who has worked the longest at the studio, I used the MAX function on the years_employed column.

2. To calculate the average years worked for each role, I grouped the rows by role and used the AVG function.

3. To find the total number of years worked in each building, I grouped the rows by building and summed the years_employed values.

## What I learned
This exercise helped me understand how aggregate functions like MAX, AVG, and SUM work together with GROUP BY to analyze data in tables.
