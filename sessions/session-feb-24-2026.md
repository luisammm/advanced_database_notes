## What I understood

- Aggregate functions allow us to summarize data instead of looking at every individual row.
- Functions like MAX, AVG, and SUM help answer questions about totals, averages, or extreme values in a dataset.
- GROUP BY is important because it lets us apply these calculations to categories, such as roles or buildings.
- This makes it easier to analyze patterns in the data rather than just listing records.
