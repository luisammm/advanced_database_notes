## What I understood

### KPI Dashboards

A KPI dashboard is a collection of metrics that helps teams monitor performance and make decisions. A good dashboard should answer specific business questions instead of simply displaying data.

### Business Metrics

Metrics should have a clear definition and a clear purpose. Different definitions can produce different results, so consistency is important when calculating KPIs.

### SQL Aggregations

Aggregate functions such as COUNT, AVG, SUM, MIN, and MAX are commonly used to generate dashboard metrics. These functions help summarize large amounts of information.

### Dashboard Design

A dashboard becomes more useful when information is grouped by categories such as status, priority, team, or time period. This makes trends easier to identify.

### KPI Quality

Not every metric is a good KPI. A useful KPI should be measurable, understandable, and connected to a business objective.
